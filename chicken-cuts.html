Chicken Cuts Guide

A guide to chicken cuts, from boneless to karahi cut, and what each is best used for.

Common Chicken Cuts We Offer

Boneless chicken and chicken breast are popular for karahi, handi and grilled dishes. Karahi cut and biryani cut are bone-in pieces sized for those specific recipes, and chicken qeema is ground fresh for qeema paratha and kebabs.
