About Boneless Chicken

Boneless chicken is our most-ordered chicken cut, and for good reason — it saves prep time in the kitchen and cooks quickly and evenly since there's no bone slowing down heat transfer. We remove the skin and bone by hand from whole chicken so what reaches your door is clean, trimmed meat ready to go straight into the pan. It's the cut most Karachi households reach for when they want a fast weeknight karahi or a stir-fry without the extra work of deboning at home.

Cut & Portion Information

Boneless chicken is cut from the breast and leg portions and supplied as medium cubes or thick strips, depending on what you request when ordering. A 1kg pack typically yields enough boneless meat for a family-sized karahi or handi for 4–5 people. If you need a specific cut size — small cubes for a quick stir-fry, or larger chunks for a slow-cooked handi — just mention it on WhatsApp and we'll prepare it accordingly.

Boneless & Skinless — What This Means for Cooking

This cut is fully boneless and skinless. Removing the skin reduces excess fat and makes the dish lighter, while removing the bone means every piece cooks at the same rate — there's no risk of undercooked meat near a bone or overcooked edges waiting for bone-in pieces to finish. If you specifically want skin-on boneless chicken for extra flavor, let us know when ordering on WhatsApp and we can prepare it that way.

Minimum Order & Preparation Options

Minimum order for boneless chicken is 1kg. We can prepare it as medium cubes (the standard cut for karahi and handi), thin strips (for stir-fry and Chinese-style dishes), or butterflied pieces on request. Tell us the dish you're cooking and we'll suggest the right cut size.

Recommended Dishes

Boneless chicken works well in chicken karahi, chicken handi, chicken stir-fry, Chinese-style chicken dishes, chicken sandwiches and wraps, and quick curries. Because it cooks fast and evenly, it's also the go-to choice for weeknight meals where you don't have hours to spend in the kitchen.

Cooking Recommendations

Since there's no bone to slow down cooking, boneless chicken cooks noticeably faster than bone-in cuts — usually within 12–15 minutes on medium-high heat once the masala base is ready. Avoid cooking it for too long on high heat, as boneless, skinless chicken can dry out quickly; add it toward the later stage of your gravy and simmer just until cooked through. Marinating for 20–30 minutes beforehand helps keep it moist and adds flavor.

Storage & Freshness

Boneless chicken is prepared fresh on the day of delivery and should ideally be cooked the same day for best taste and texture. If you're not cooking it immediately, refrigerate it and use within 24 hours, or freeze it in an airtight bag for up to one month. Thaw fully in the refrigerator, not at room temperature, before cooking.

Boneless Chicken — Frequently Asked Questions

Is boneless chicken skinless as well?

Yes, by default boneless chicken is prepared skin-off. If you'd prefer skin-on boneless chicken, let us know when you place your order on WhatsApp.

What's the minimum order for boneless chicken?

The minimum order is 1kg. You can order in whole or half-kilogram quantities above that.

Can I get boneless chicken cut into strips instead of cubes?

Yes. Just mention on WhatsApp whether you'd like cubes, strips or butterflied pieces and we'll prepare it to that size.

How long does boneless chicken stay fresh after delivery?

It's best cooked the same day. If not, refrigerate and use within 24 hours, or freeze for up to a month.
