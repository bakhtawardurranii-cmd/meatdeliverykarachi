Meat Delivery in DHA Karachi — All Phases

DHA is spread across eight separate phases, and the butcher near Phase 1 is no help if you live in Phase 8 or the newer Phase 6 extension. We deliver fresh, Zabiha Halal meat directly to your house in every DHA phase, so distance inside DHA is never the problem.

Fresh Meat Delivery in DHA — Cut to Order

DHA Karachi isn't one neighbourhood — it's eight phases spread over a very large area, and each one developed at a different time with a different mix of shops. Older phases like Phase 1 and Phase 2 have long-established butchers, but newer sectors — parts of Phase 6, Phase 7 and Phase 8 — often don't have a shop residents fully trust yet, or it's simply a long drive across DHA to reach one.

We treat that as our starting point: wherever your house sits — any phase, any sector — we deliver the same fresh-cut, Zabiha Halal chicken, beef and mutton to your gate. Bungalow living in DHA also tends to mean bigger orders — a family barbecue or a Sunday karahi for extended family — so we're used to preparing larger quantities in one go, not just single-kg orders.

Pricing is the same across every phase: chicken (chest, leg and mix) Rs. 1,499/kg, beef Rs. 2,099/kg bone-in or Rs. 3,099/kg boneless, and mutton Rs. 4,499/kg — with free delivery to any DHA phase, no minimum order.
