About Mutton Qeema

Mutton qeema is ground fresh from trimmed mutton once your order is confirmed, giving you the rich, slightly fattier flavor mutton is known for in a minced form that's quick to cook. It's a step up in richness from chicken or beef qeema and a popular choice for dishes where mutton's distinct flavor is specifically wanted, such as qeema karahi.

Cut & Portion Information

Mutton qeema is ground from trimmed mutton, supplied by weight. A 1kg order is enough for a qeema karahi, kofta batch, or keema paratha filling serving 4–5 people. We can adjust the fat content slightly if you'd prefer a leaner or richer grind.

Boneless, Ground Fresh to Order

Mutton qeema is entirely boneless, as it's ground meat. Being minced fresh from trimmed cuts, there's nothing further to trim or debone — it's ready to season and cook as soon as it arrives.

Minimum Order & Preparation Options

Minimum order for mutton qeema is 1kg. We grind mutton qeema fresh once your order is placed rather than from pre-minced stock. On request, we can grind it finer for kofta or coarser for a chunkier qeema karahi texture.

Recommended Dishes

Mutton qeema is used for qeema karahi, kofta curry, keema paratha, and other minced-mutton dishes where mutton's richer flavor is preferred over chicken or beef qeema.

Cooking Recommendations

Cook mutton qeema on medium-high heat, breaking it up well as it cooks. Mutton qeema tends to release more fat than chicken qeema, so you can drain some off partway through if you prefer a leaner final dish. Because mutton has a stronger flavor, it pairs well with well-toasted whole spices and a longer simmer to let the flavors develop fully.

Storage & Freshness

Minced meat has a shorter shelf life than whole cuts — cook mutton qeema within 24 hours of delivery, or freeze it immediately if not using it the same day. Frozen mutton qeema keeps for up to two months; thaw in the refrigerator before cooking.
