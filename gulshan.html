Meat Delivery in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi

Gulshan-e-Iqbal is one of Karachi's most densely populated areas, and the traffic around NIPA Chowrangi and University Road can turn a five-minute butcher run into a forty-minute one. We deliver fresh, Zabiha Halal meat to Block 1 through 16, so you never have to sit in that traffic for dinner.

Fresh Meat Delivery in Gulshan-e-Iqbal — Cut to Order

Gulshan-e-Iqbal is one of the busiest residential areas in Karachi, packed into a grid of numbered blocks from 1 through 16 and beyond. Every block has its own small market, but quality and hygiene vary a lot from shop to shop, and reaching a specific butcher often means driving through the constant congestion around NIPA Chowrangi or University Road first.

Because Gulshan is so block-heavy, we get asked constantly whether a specific block is covered — the answer is almost always yes. We deliver across Block 1 through 16 and the surrounding sectors, so instead of driving to whichever shop happens to be decent that week, you get the same fresh-cut, Zabiha Halal chicken, beef or mutton delivered straight to your block, every time.

Pricing is consistent across all of Gulshan-e-Iqbal: chicken (chest, leg and mix) Rs. 1,499/kg, beef Rs. 2,099/kg bone-in or Rs. 3,099/kg boneless, and mutton Rs. 4,499/kg — with free delivery to any block, no minimum order.
