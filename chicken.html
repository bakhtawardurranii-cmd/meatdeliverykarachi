Fresh Chicken Karachi — Cut to Order

Meat Delivery Karachi supplies fresh chicken online across Karachi. Every order is cut fresh against your request rather than pulled from a pre-cut display tray — so when you buy chicken online from us, it's prepared specifically for your delivery, not sitting out for hours beforehand.

We stock the chicken cuts Karachi kitchens use most: boneless chicken for karahi and stir-fry, lean chicken breast fillets for grilling, bone-in chicken karahi cut for a proper Karachi-style karahi, chicken biryani cut sized to cook evenly with rice, and freshly ground chicken qeema for kebabs, parathas and curries. If you need a different cut or preparation, just tell us on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to prepare it.

All chicken is halal, cleaned and hygienically packed before dispatch, and weight is confirmed with you before your order is finalized — so there are no surprises when it arrives at your door.
