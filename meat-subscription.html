How Our Meat Subscription Works

A meat subscription with Meat Delivery Karachi means you never have to think about placing your order again. Tell us once — which meat (chicken, beef, mutton, or a mix), how much, and how often — and we'll deliver it to your door automatically on a weekly or monthly schedule, cut fresh to order every single time.

It's built for households and small kitchens that buy meat regularly and would rather set it and forget it: no extra subscription fee, no price markup — you pay the same per-kg rate as a one-time order, and every delivery is Zabiha Halal, hygienically packed, and comes with free delivery all over Karachi.

You're always in control — pause a delivery before a trip, change the quantity, switch meat type, or cancel entirely, just by messaging us on WhatsApp.

Who is it for?

Families and households in Karachi who buy chicken, beef or mutton every week and want a reliable, hassle-free routine instead of messaging us fresh each time.

How do I start a subscription?

Message us on WhatsApp with the meat, quantity and frequency you want. We'll confirm your schedule, price and first delivery date before setting up your standing order.

Meat Subscription FAQs

What is a meat subscription?

A recurring order — weekly or monthly — where we deliver fresh, Zabiha Halal chicken, beef and/or mutton to your address on a fixed schedule, so you never have to place a new order each time.

How does the meat subscription work?

Tell us on WhatsApp which meat and quantity you want and how often. We confirm your standing order, delivery day and price, then deliver automatically on schedule. Pause, change or cancel anytime.

What does a meat subscription cost?

No extra subscription fee — you pay the same per-kg price as a regular order: chicken Rs. 1,499/kg, beef from Rs. 2,099/kg, mutton Rs. 4,499/kg, with free delivery all over Karachi.

Is subscription meat the same quality as a one-time order?

Yes. Every subscription delivery is cut fresh to order on the day of delivery, exactly like a one-time order.

Can I customize my meat subscription?

Yes — mix chicken, beef and mutton in any combination, choose specific cuts, change quantity, and adjust your delivery day anytime on WhatsApp.
