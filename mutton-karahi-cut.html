About Mutton Karahi Cut

Mutton karahi cut is bone-in mutton jointed into medium pieces suited to the karahi cooking style, where the bone and surrounding fat render slowly into the masala over a longer cook than chicken karahi requires. Mutton is naturally tougher than chicken, so getting the piece size right matters even more here — too large and the outside can dry out before the inside is tender; too small and you lose the texture mutton karahi is known for.

Cut & Portion Information

Mutton karahi cut is jointed into medium bone-in pieces, generally 8–10 pieces per kilogram. A 1kg order typically serves 3–4 people as a main course, similar to chicken karahi cut, though mutton is often served with slightly smaller portions per person given its richness.

Bone-In for Traditional Karahi Flavor

Mutton karahi cut is bone-in by default, which is essential to how mutton karahi is traditionally prepared — the bone marrow and surrounding fat render into the gravy over the longer cooking time mutton needs, building a depth of flavor a boneless cut wouldn't achieve in the same way.

Minimum Order & Preparation Options

Minimum order for mutton karahi cut is 1kg. Pieces are jointed to a medium karahi size as standard. We can cut them slightly smaller if you're short on cooking time, though we'd note this may affect how much the bone contributes to the final gravy.

Recommended Dishes

This cut is made for mutton karahi and mutton handi, and works well in any bone-in mutton curry where a rich, slow-developed gravy is the goal.

Cooking Recommendations

Mutton karahi cut needs considerably longer than chicken — typically 45–60 minutes of simmering, or 20–25 minutes in a pressure cooker, until the meat is tender and pulling away from the bone. Cook covered on medium heat for most of the process to let the bone-in pieces cook through evenly, then uncover toward the end to reduce and thicken the gravy.

Storage & Freshness

Refrigerate immediately after delivery and use within 1–2 days, or freeze for up to two months in an airtight bag. Thaw fully in the refrigerator before cooking so the pieces cook evenly.
