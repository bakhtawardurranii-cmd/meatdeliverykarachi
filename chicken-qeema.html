About Chicken Qeema

Chicken qeema is boneless chicken ground fresh at the time of your order, rather than pre-minced and left sitting — minced meat spoils faster than whole cuts, so we grind it only once your order is confirmed. It's a lighter alternative to beef or mutton qeema and a popular choice for households that want the same range of qeema dishes with less red meat.

Cut & Portion Information

Chicken qeema is ground from boneless chicken (breast and leg meat combined for a balanced fat ratio) and supplied by weight. A 1kg order is enough for a qeema curry or a batch of kebabs/parathas serving 4–5 people. We can adjust the leanness slightly — leg meat gives a juicier qeema, breast meat gives a leaner one — just mention your preference.

Boneless, Ground Fresh to Order

Chicken qeema is entirely boneless, as it's ground meat rather than a whole cut. Because there's no bone or skin involved, there's nothing to trim or debone on your end — it's ready to season and cook straight away.

Minimum Order & Preparation Options

Minimum order for chicken qeema is 1kg. We grind chicken qeema fresh once your order is placed, rather than keeping pre-minced stock. This means slightly longer prep time on our end, but noticeably fresher qeema on your plate. On request, we can grind it coarser or finer depending on the dish.

Recommended Dishes

Chicken qeema is used for qeema paratha, chicken kebabs and shami kebabs, qeema curry, stuffed dishes like qeema-stuffed capsicum or samosas, and as a lighter alternative in any recipe that traditionally uses beef or mutton qeema.

Cooking Recommendations

Cook chicken qeema on medium heat and break it up well as it cooks to avoid clumping. Since it's leaner than beef or mutton qeema, it can dry out if left on high heat for too long — add your spices and a little oil early to keep it moist, and avoid over-frying once the water content has cooked off.

Storage & Freshness

Minced meat has a shorter shelf life than whole cuts, so chicken qeema should be cooked within 24 hours of delivery, or frozen immediately if not using it the same day. Frozen chicken qeema keeps well for up to one month — thaw in the refrigerator, not at room temperature, before cooking.
