About Boneless Beef

Boneless beef is trimmed of excess fat and sinew and cut into cubes or chunks, giving you meat that's ready to marinate and cook without any prep work on your side. It's a versatile, everyday cut that works across a wide range of Karachi home cooking, from a quick beef karahi to a slower stew, without the extra step of deboning that bone-in beef requires.

Cut & Portion Information

Boneless beef is cut from trimmed beef cuts into medium cubes or chunks, supplied by weight. A 1kg order typically serves 4–5 people in a curry or karahi. We can prepare it as smaller cubes for a quick-cooking karahi or larger chunks for a slow-braised stew — let us know when ordering.

Fully Boneless

Boneless beef is, as the name suggests, completely bone-free — all bone and heavy connective tissue is removed before cutting. This makes it faster to cook and easier to portion than bone-in cuts, though it won't produce quite as thick a gravy as bone-in beef used for dishes like nihari, where the bone and connective tissue are specifically wanted.

Minimum Order & Preparation Options

Minimum order for boneless beef is 1kg. We supply boneless beef as medium cubes by default. On request, we can cut it into smaller pieces for a quicker karahi, or thicker chunks for slow-cooked stews and curries where the meat needs to hold together over a longer cook time.

Recommended Dishes

Boneless beef works well in beef karahi, beef stew, beef curry, bhuna gosht, and any recipe calling for boneless beef chunks rather than minced or bone-in meat.

Cooking Recommendations

Boneless beef benefits from slower cooking to become tender — beef is tougher than chicken and needs more time for the connective tissue to break down. Braise or simmer on low-medium heat for at least 45–60 minutes, or use a pressure cooker to speed this up. Searing the cubes first on high heat before adding liquid helps build flavor into the final dish.

Storage & Freshness

Refrigerate immediately after delivery and use within 1–2 days for best quality. Boneless beef freezes well for up to two months in an airtight bag — thaw in the refrigerator before cooking rather than at room temperature.

Boneless Beef — Frequently Asked Questions

Is boneless beef the same as beef used for nihari?

No, boneless beef is a general-purpose cut for karahi, stew and curry. Nihari uses our beef nihari cut, which includes shank and connective-tissue-rich pieces for a thicker gravy.

How long does boneless beef take to cook?

Generally 45–60 minutes of simmering or braising, longer than chicken, since beef needs more time to become tender.

Can I get smaller cubes for a quick karahi?

Yes, just mention this on WhatsApp when ordering and we'll cut it smaller.

How long can I store boneless beef before cooking?

Use within 1–2 days if refrigerated, or freeze for up to two months.
