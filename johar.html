Fresh Meat Delivery in Gulistan-e-Jauhar — Cut to Order

Gulistan-e-Jauhar is one of the fastest-growing parts of Karachi, now stretching across Block 1 through 19 and still expanding with new housing societies. The older blocks near the original core have had butchers for years, but if you've recently moved into one of the newer sectors further out, there often isn't a shop nearby that you already know and trust.

That gap is exactly what we cover. Whether you're in an established block near Millennium Mall and the UBL Sports Complex or in a newer society further from the main road, we deliver the same fresh-cut, Zabiha Halal chicken, beef and mutton to your door — so being in a newer part of Jauhar doesn't mean settling for whatever meat shop happens to have opened nearby.

Pricing is the same across all of Gulistan-e-Jauhar: chicken (chest, leg and mix) Rs. 1,499/kg, beef Rs. 2,099/kg bone-in or Rs. 3,099/kg boneless, and mutton Rs. 4,499/kg — with free delivery to every block, no minimum order.
