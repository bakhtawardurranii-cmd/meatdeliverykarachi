Fresh Beef Karachi — Cut to Order

Meat Delivery Karachi supplies fresh beef online across Karachi. Every order is cut fresh against your request rather than pulled from a pre-cut display tray, so when you buy beef online from us, it's prepared specifically for your delivery.

We stock the beef cuts Karachi kitchens use most: boneless beef, trimmed into cubes and chunks for karahi, stew or slow-cooked curries; freshly ground beef qeema for kofta, qeema matar and samosa filling; beef nihari cut — shank and connective-tissue-rich pieces selected for slow-cooked nihari; beef haleem cut, small even pieces that shred properly during a long cook; and beef BBQ cuts — boti and seekh-ready pieces trimmed for chapli kebab, boti and seekh kebab. If you need a different cut, tell us on WhatsApp and we'll do our best to prepare it.

All beef is halal, cleaned and hygienically packed before dispatch, and weight is confirmed with you before your order is finalized.

Availability

All beef cuts listed below are prepared fresh to order and available daily. Message us your required cut and quantity, and we'll confirm stock, weight and price before preparing it.

Karachi Delivery Information

Beef orders are delivered across Karachi, including North Nazimabad, all phases of DHA, Clifton, Korangi, Shah Faisal Town, all blocks of Gulshan-e-Iqbal, all blocks of Gulistan-e-Jauhar and PECHS. Delivery time depends on your location and order size — we confirm an estimated window with you on WhatsApp or by call before dispatch.
