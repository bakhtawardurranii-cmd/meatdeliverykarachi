About Beef BBQ Cuts

Our beef BBQ cuts are trimmed specifically for grilling — leaner and more evenly cut than a general boneless beef order, so skewers cook evenly and don't turn tough or chewy over open flame. We prepare it as cubes for boti or coarsely minced for seekh kebab, depending on what you order, so you're not doing extra trimming or mincing before your BBQ.

Cut & Portion Information

Beef BBQ cuts are supplied either as boti cubes (for skewered boti and chapli kebab) or minced meat (for seekh kebab), both trimmed of excess fat for a cleaner grill. A 1kg order of boti generally makes 15–18 skewer pieces, enough for 4–5 people at a barbecue.

Boneless, Trimmed for Grilling

Beef BBQ cuts are always boneless, since skewered and minced BBQ preparations need clean, bone-free meat. We trim larger pieces of fat that would otherwise flare up and burn on the grill, while keeping enough marbling for flavor and moisture during cooking.

Minimum Order & Preparation Options

Minimum order for beef bbq cuts is 1kg. Tell us whether you need boti cubes (for skewers) or minced meat (for seekh kebab and chapli kebab) when ordering — we prepare each differently, so this needs to be specified in advance rather than adjusted after cutting.

Recommended Dishes

This cut is made for beef boti, chapli kebab, seekh kebab, and any barbecue or grilled beef dish where the meat is skewered or shaped by hand before cooking over direct heat.

Cooking Recommendations

Marinate boti cubes for at least 2–4 hours (overnight for best results) before grilling, using a yogurt-based marinade to help tenderize the meat. Grill on high heat for a good sear, turning regularly, and avoid overcooking, as beef BBQ cuts can dry out quickly once past medium doneness. For seekh kebab, keep the mince well-chilled before shaping so it holds together on the skewer.

Storage & Freshness

Refrigerate immediately and use within 1–2 days, or marinate and freeze for up to a month — marinating before freezing actually helps deepen the flavor for BBQ cuts. Thaw in the refrigerator before grilling.

Beef BBQ Cuts — Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between beef BBQ cut and boneless beef?

Beef BBQ cut is trimmed and portioned specifically for grilling — leaner cuts for boti, or mince for seekh kebab — while boneless beef is a general-purpose cut for karahi and curry.

Do you prepare it as cubes or minced?

Both — just tell us on WhatsApp whether you need boti cubes for skewers or minced meat for seekh kebab.

How long should I marinate beef BBQ cuts?

At least 2–4 hours, ideally overnight, for the best tenderness and flavor.

Can beef BBQ cuts be frozen after marinating?

Yes, marinating before freezing is fine and can even help deepen the flavor — freeze for up to one month.
