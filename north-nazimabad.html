Meat Delivery in North Nazimabad, Karachi

North Nazimabad's block markets are convenient, but freshness by early evening isn't always consistent, and larger joint-family households often need one bigger order instead of several small trips. We deliver fresh, Zabiha Halal meat across every block in North Nazimabad, cut fresh the same day.

Fresh Meat Delivery in North Nazimabad — Cut to Order

North Nazimabad is one of Karachi's oldest planned residential towns, laid out in a grid of numbered blocks around hubs like Five Star Chowrangi and Sakhi Hassan. It has no shortage of block-level meat shops, but by late afternoon, stock at a small local shop can already be sitting out for hours — and many North Nazimabad households are larger, joint-family setups where one small daily trip doesn't cover what's actually needed for the evening meal.

We deliver fresh-cut, Zabiha Halal chicken, beef and mutton to any block in North Nazimabad, prepared against your order rather than sitting on a counter — so freshness doesn't depend on what time of day you happen to send someone out. It also means one order can cover a full family's needs in one delivery instead of several separate trips.

Pricing is the same across North Nazimabad: chicken (chest, leg and mix) Rs. 1,499/kg, beef Rs. 2,099/kg bone-in or Rs. 3,099/kg boneless, and mutton Rs. 4,499/kg — with free delivery to every block, no minimum order.
