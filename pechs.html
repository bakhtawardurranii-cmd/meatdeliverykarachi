Meat Delivery in PECHS, Karachi

PECHS sits right next to Tariq Road, which sounds convenient until you actually need to park there. We deliver fresh, Zabiha Halal meat to Blocks 2 through 6, so you get your chicken, beef or mutton without fighting Tariq Road traffic and crowds for it.

Fresh Meat Delivery in PECHS — Cut to Order

PECHS is a well-established, mostly bungalow-and-apartment neighbourhood spread across Blocks 2 through 6, sitting right beside one of Karachi's busiest commercial strips — Tariq Road. That location cuts both ways: shops are close by, but Tariq Road's traffic and parking make even a short meat run a genuine hassle, especially in the evening rush.

We deliver fresh-cut, Zabiha Halal chicken, beef and mutton directly to homes across PECHS Block 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, so you get the convenience of being near Tariq Road without actually needing to park or walk through it. Many of our regular PECHS customers are older residents or working professionals who'd simply rather not deal with the crowds for a routine meat order.

Pricing is the same across PECHS: chicken (chest, leg and mix) Rs. 1,499/kg, beef Rs. 2,099/kg bone-in or Rs. 3,099/kg boneless, and mutton Rs. 4,499/kg — with free delivery to every block, no minimum order.
